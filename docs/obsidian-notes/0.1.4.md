askMyu 0.1.4 (open beta)

A meeting's page becomes Myu's brief with your own list and notes in it, Myu asks who people are with buttons, and a vault whose key is not the account's is asked to connect again.

Added
- A meeting's page is Myu's brief, with your own list inside it. Add an item, tick it, change its words or its place, or take it off, on the page or under the meeting's row in Today. Myu suggests items; each is one tap to add, and Myu never adds one by itself.
- Notes during a meeting. While a meeting is on, its page has a notes box, saved as you type. After the meeting Myu reads them for its report, and × takes off anything it read wrong. The reminder it offers has Yes and ×.
- After a meeting, its page is the meeting's record: what was decided, what you owe and what is owed to you, each with where it came from.
- Who someone is. When Myu is not sure who a name is, it asks in Today with buttons ("Is tessc-gh on GitHub Tess Colleague?"), and you can pick someone else or type a name. Undo puts it back as it was.
- "Same person" on two people says who the one person will be before it joins them. Undo puts them back.
- When Myu's question is about someone with a LinkedIn profile, the profile is linked under it.
- Lines about your week: "Myu: your week." with what the week held; a whole day under one line; a reply that bears on something you were told, with "Add it?"; a promise of yours that someone else's decision affects; several messages Myu drafted under one yes, each shown in full.
- Charts in saved canvases and in Myu's folder are written as a table, with a line saying where the numbers came from.
- Your own card has two new sections about how you are developing. Dates on cards read as days ("28 Sep", with the year when it is not this year).
- A space you cannot open yet, because someone has to let you in first, says who you are waiting for.
- Signing in with an emailed link at an address where an account was made with a password and never confirmed asks one question: that account's password, to open it.
- Creating an account at an address that already has one says so, without saying whose it is.

Changed
- A vault whose key does not open your account is asked to connect again, and the key it held is set aside on this device, never deleted, so your things can be brought back if that was wrong. Until you connect again it does not use that key or send it anywhere.
- When the server cannot check this vault just now, or another of your devices is still setting up, the plugin says so in plain words and waits before it tries again.
- The README lists the setting for how far back Myu reads your mail, the pages that open from Myu's questions and a meeting's page, and every copy button.
