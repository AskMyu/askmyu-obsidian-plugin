askMyu 0.1.8 (open beta)

Myu's log and your promises now go by your own day, in your own time zone.

Added
- A promise due at a time says it, for example "by 15:00 tomorrow"; one tied to a meeting says which, for example "at Sizing review (17:34 today)".

Changed
- Myu's log is easier to read: it is grouped into what Myu did for you, what it told you and what it is holding, and a check that found nothing new is folded into one line.
- Myu's log notes now run from midnight where you are. Before, a day's note ran from midnight in universal time (UTC), so an evening could land in the next day's note. The first time after the update, yesterday's and today's notes are written again, so no evening goes missing; a note you edited is left as it is, and today's note then picks up from where it ended.
- A row from another day shows its day, for example "Yesterday 21:00". Rows Myu cannot place come last, under "Other".
- When a promise is next checked is said in your time zone.

Fixed
- Words that came from someone's message stay on one line in Myu's log and cannot add headings, embeds, tags or other formatting to your notes.
