askMyu 0.1.6 (open beta)

A message to send goes the way it was shown, a message already being sent says so, and days are named as they were set.

Added
- Every answer on a message to send carries the way it was shown going. If that way has changed, the line shows Myu's sentence and how it goes now, and asks again.
- Pressing a message that is already being sent says "This is being sent. Give it a moment." and Today is read again a moment later.

Changed
- "Not now" and "Don't ask again" follow Myu's own settings for each choice. A late promise shows "Not now" again beside "Give it another week", and choices such as "Not me" or "Decline" count as the line's own way out.
- "What you owe" names the day of each next check as it was set, wherever you are. A promise with no check of its own shows its due day, and a check day that has passed reads "the check was Friday 20 November".
- When a line's second line is a heading, the first thing under it shows at most two lines before "read all" unless it is a bullet.
- A message Myu writes in an answer is shown once, with who it goes to.

Fixed
- A press Myu refuses but keeps (for example a button the line no longer has) shows Myu's sentence and the line as it is now, instead of "Could not send this".
