askMyu 0.1.3 (open beta)

Spaces come to Obsidian, Myu's questions are answered with buttons, the Today pane is simpler, and the wording across the plugin is plainer.

Added
- Myu's questions in Today and in a person's conversation have buttons. A yes/no question shows Yes and No. A question with set answers shows them. Every question also has "Not now" (it comes back tomorrow) and "Don't ask again". A question that asks you to pick several people shows each as a toggle, with a box for a name not on the list.
- "What you owe" under Today: what you owe people and what they owe you, folded behind one line. A row opens that person's conversation.
- A meeting has its own conversation with Myu. The meeting's card (the pane that opens before a meeting) was rebuilt: what the meeting is for, since last time, your moves, a block per person, what to know before you go in, and Myu's question before the meeting, answered on the card.
- Interview debriefs. A debrief space has its own conversation with Myu, like a retro's, and a row in Today. The interview time and the date the scorecards open show on the calendar beside your synced events.
- `Myu/Me.md`: your own card, written into Myu's folder with the other files.
- A person's card shows how things stand in plain words (going well, steady, worth a check-in, needs some care), and dates on its timeline as words.
- People who were only on some of your emails are kept out of the people list. The look-up's last line says how many there are and opens the list. "Keep track" on one of them brings them back. Search still finds them.
- Pasted text. A long paste in a chat box becomes a block beside your words instead of being cut. "Ask Myu about this note" and "about this selection" send the note or the selection the same way.
- Files on a feedback report. "Add a file" attaches up to five files from your device, 10 MB each, 15 MB in all. They go to the team by email and nowhere else. A reply rating attaches the conversation's transcript, and you can leave it out.
- When a LinkedIn address you paste names a profile in another name than your account's, the plugin asks whether it is yours before it goes on the account.
- The status bar shows what is new since you last looked, for each of Myu's places ("myu · 3 new in Today · 2 in Spaces"). Opening a place clears its count on every device. On a phone, where there is no status bar, the same words sit at the top of the Today pane.
- The plugin saves this computer's time zone to your account when it differs from the saved one, so the morning brief follows your own day.
- The password for new devices. If you set one in askMyu, the connect window asks for it first. Type it and the vault has the key. Approving from another device and the recovery phrase stay as the other two ways in.
- Spaces. Every space you belong to now has a row under "Spaces" in Today, whether or not Myu has anything to say about it that day. The row opens that space's own conversation with Myu. Myu asks one question at a time. The text box is always there, and a question with set answers shows them as buttons.
- The team's retro. A space's pane has two tabs: "Your conversation" and "The team's retro". The retro tab is a page for reading: where the retro stands, each question with what people wrote, the summary the team chose, and the tasks. Voting, accepting and taking a task happen in your conversation, where Myu asks, and the page ends with a button back to it. "See the team's retro ▸" on the space's row in Today opens that tab directly.
- A status line under the space's title says where the space's retro stands right now.
- If a space is locked in this session, the pane shows an Unlock button. You stay where you are.
- When Myu asks in a space for your LinkedIn profile or a resume, or to see your calendar, the pane says where to do that on the web, with the link. The plugin does not upload files or open the calendar consent itself.
- When something you wrote in a space fits your journal better, "Take it there" opens a new journal conversation with your text in the box. Nothing is sent until you send it.
- A person page's list of shared spaces opens the space here in Obsidian. It used to link to the web and say spaces were web only.
- Myu's folder: person pages have new sections, including your spaces with that person, commitments as checkboxes, and what Myu did about them. `Myu/Today.md` lists what needs your attention today. A new daily log at `Myu/Log/<day>.md` lists what Myu read, assumed, said, did and is waiting on.

Changed
- A line of Myu's counts as seen once it has been on screen for a second. The plugin tells the server, so an interview debrief Myu drafted can send its invitations after you have seen it.
- Where a memory came from opens as a window with the email or journal entry and the memories Myu drew from it.
- Today is one list. Each person, company, space and meeting is one row. The row shows the one thing Myu has to say about it, with `talk about this ▸` to open a conversation about it, and `prepare ▸` on your next meeting. If Myu has more to say about the same person, the row says how many ("12 more — show ▸") and opens in place. Rows that need you come first. After the first five, the rest sit behind "Show 30 more ▸", so a busy day does not push your meetings off the pane.
- `Myu/Today.md` follows the same rule: one item per person or space, with their name, the line that matters now, and how many more there are.
- A space's conversation shows one question at a time. A question the server holds back until another is answered stays out of the pane, Today and `Myu/Today.md` until its turn.
- What is open with you today shows as checkboxes. The old cue rows, the "this week" rows and the per-item Open buttons are gone.
- Words typed in a space go to that space's conversation. Before, they went into a journal conversation and the space never saw them.
- Wording across the plugin is plainer: buttons, settings, the sign-up and consent screens.
- The README lists every network call the plugin can make, including the new ones for spaces, Today, seen lines, the status bar's counts, Myu's calendar entries and correcting a person, and a few older account and canvas calls that were missing from the list.

Fixed
- Today's "Help Myu" rows read from the current endpoint. The old one was removed from the server.
