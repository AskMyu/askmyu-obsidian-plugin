AskMyu 0.0.246 (beta)

Install with BRAT: AskMyu/askmyu-obsidian-plugin

New
- Weave Myu in: one button in Settings opens a page of ready-to-paste snippets for your daily notes, weekly note, Tasks and Dataview. Each has a copy button.
- Insert a Myu snippet: a new command that drops one of those snippets at your cursor.
- Myu look: install AskMyu's optional look from Settings → Advanced. Turn it off or remove it from the same place. Nothing is downloaded.

Fixed
- Settings could show "Connect…" for a Google account that was already connected, or "No other devices" when you had several. If something can't load, Settings now says so and gives you a Retry button.
- If your session expired, the plugin could get stuck until you restarted Obsidian. It now reconnects on its own.
- Settings sections no longer shuffle around after you click something.
- The Account section now reads top to bottom: devices, email addresses, your name, what Myu calls you, and delete account last.
- In a narrow Settings window, text no longer gets squeezed to one word per line.
