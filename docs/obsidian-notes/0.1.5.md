askMyu 0.1.5 (open beta)

Long lines show short first, a message Myu cannot send says so once and can be copied, and Disconnect ends this vault's sign-in on the server.

Added
- Long lines in Today show their first two lines, then "read all" to open the rest in place. A line whose second line is a heading ("That affects something you promised:") also shows the first thing under it. A line that sends a message always shows whole.
- When Myu cannot send a message (nothing connected can carry it), its line offers "Copy it" and "Drop it". "Copy it" opens the message and puts it on the clipboard; its button then reads "Copied".
- Under a message Myu wrote, what you must see before using it: for example "I can't send mail for you yet." and "To Carla Diaz at carla@example.test.".
- A line that tells you something can carry a choice of its own, for example "Not so, keep it open" when Myu closed something.
- After too many wrong tries, the question says how long to wait and stays, to be answered again after.

Changed
- "From Myu" leads with the first item for today. The rest is behind "+N more". A count of new mail is never the first thing. With nothing for today, it says how many things need your answer, or "Nothing needs your answer today."
- "Not now" and "Don't ask again" show only where a line's own choices do not already say them: no "Don't ask again" beside "Drop it" or "Let it go", no "Not now" beside "Give it another week", and neither on a message to send.
- A promise someone else's decision affects shows Tell, Let it go and Keep it as buttons, the same as other questions.
- "Copy" is "Copy it".
- A drafted message no longer says it goes somewhere when it cannot be sent, and "can't send" is said once.
- A line you answer here keeps its place, with Myu's reply under it, for as long as Today shows it.

Fixed
- Disconnect ends this vault's sign-in on the server first (`POST /account/logout`, listed in the README), then forgets the key on this device.
- Line breaks inside Myu's lines are kept.
